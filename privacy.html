Privacy Policy

1. Scope and Application

This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, stored, disclosed, and processed through the ATLAY mobile application and related services (the “Services”). ATLAY is operated by the individual developer identified in its official Apple App Store and Google Play listings (the “Developer”).

2. Information We Process

Depending on features used, ATLAY may process account identifiers and authentication information; prompts, conversation history and AI responses; saved memories; voice recordings, transcriptions and synthesized speech; submitted and generated images; location information with applicable permission; alarms, reminders, tasks, notes and calendar-related records; device identifiers, usage credits, technical logs and security events. Information may be held on the device, in application infrastructure, or by service providers.

3. Purposes and Legal Bases

Information is processed to authenticate users, provide AI and organization features, maintain history and supported personalization, manage service allowances, prevent abuse, troubleshoot problems and comply with legal obligations. Where required, a valid legal basis may include contract performance, legal obligations, legitimate interests or consent. The precise basis for each processing activity must be verified before publication.

4. Third-Party Providers

ATLAY uses Clerk for authentication and Supabase for application infrastructure and storage. Nova text, relevant conversation context, voice transcription, speech generation and memory embeddings are processed using OpenAI services. Council processes requests through the selected provider—OpenAI, Anthropic or Google Gemini—and supports image generation through available providers. Nova does not generate images. Weather requests may use OpenWeather; internet search may use OpenAI, Tavily or Serper where enabled. The information shared depends on the feature and may include messages, context, audio, images or request details. ATLAY does not integrate Gmail, Google Calendar or Google Contacts. External providers may retain information under their own applicable arrangements.

5. AI Processing

When you use Nova or Council, relevant messages, conversation context, saved memory information, images or audio may be transmitted through ATLAY infrastructure to the applicable external AI provider to process your request. The current app does not provide a separate pre-send AI-provider disclosure or permission screen. Device permissions, where requested, do not necessarily cover every external data-processing purpose. Avoid including unnecessary sensitive personal information.

6. History and Memory

Supported conversation history and memory may be retained to enable continuity. Disabling memory does not necessarily erase previously stored records. Individual deletion functions vary by released version and must be tested before they are represented as operational.

7. Storage and Retention

Data may be retained in application databases, device storage, technical logs, backups and provider systems. A universal automatic expiration period has not been established. Retention depends on processing purpose, account status, legal obligations and provider arrangements. Specific retention periods or criteria must be verified and disclosed where legally required.

8. Account Deletion and Requests

The app includes a Delete Account control, and the public Account Deletion page provides an email-based request method. Sending an email does not automatically delete an account. The in-app deletion process is designed to remove the ATLAY profile, conversations, memories, reminders, credit records and related application database records, then delete the Clerk sign-in account and clear specified local records. Successful end-to-end deletion has not been verified in production. Independent provider copies are not deleted through this process; backups, administrative audit identifiers and certain iOS AlarmKit metadata may remain. Users may contact the Developer to request access, correction, deletion or other applicable privacy rights, subject to appropriate verification and legal exceptions.

9. Security

ATLAY uses authentication, access controls and other safeguards intended to protect information. No system can guarantee absolute security. Technical and organizational safeguards remain subject to review.

10. Device Permissions

Depending on the feature, ATLAY may request microphone, camera, photo-library, location or notification permissions. Users can manage device permissions in system settings. Denying permission may prevent the related feature from operating.

11. International Processing

Service providers may process information across different jurisdictions. Applicable international-transfer mechanisms and safeguards must be established and verified where required.

12. Children’s Privacy

ATLAY is a general-purpose AI application and is not specifically designed for young children. Applicable age, consent, transparency and safeguarding requirements must be reflected in the final service and store declarations.

13. Advertising and Sales

The currently documented application does not use an advertising-supported business model or implemented advertising-tracking functionality. The Developer does not sell personal information to advertisers. Operational disclosures to service providers may still occur as described above.

14. Privacy Rights and Complaints

Depending on applicable law, users may have rights of access, correction, erasure, restriction, objection, portability and withdrawal of consent. Users may also be entitled to complain to a competent data-protection authority. Rights are subject to applicable conditions and exceptions.

15. Changes

This Policy may change to reflect changes to the Services or legal requirements. Updated versions will show a revision date. Notice or additional consent will be provided when required.
