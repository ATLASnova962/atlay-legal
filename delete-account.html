Request Account Deletion

To request deletion of your ATLAY account and associated personal information, send an email to atlasnova962@gmail.com with the subject ATLAY Account Deletion Request.

How to submit your request

  1. Write to us from the email address associated with your ATLAY account, if available.
  2. State that you want to delete your ATLAY account.
  3. We may ask for information necessary to verify account ownership. Do not send passwords or authentication codes.

Emailing this address submits a request for manual review; it does not automatically delete the account. The signed-in app also includes a Delete Account control. That process is designed to remove the ATLAY profile, Nova and Council conversations, memories, reminders, credits and related application database records, followed by the sign-in account and specified local records. It has not been verified end to end in production. Copies held independently by OpenAI, Anthropic or Google are not removed by this process. Backups, administrative audit identifiers and certain on-device alarm metadata may remain. If you use a Test account without an associated email address, explain that in your request; do not send a password or authentication code.

Account deletion requests may require verification of account ownership. Some information may be retained where permitted or required by applicable law. Contact atlasnova962@gmail.com for questions about your request.
